- In commutative diagrams asserting an arrow is unique, use dotted
  (**NOT** dashed) lines to represent this. Dashed lines in Algebraic
  Geometry mean "rational map of varieties", which is something very
  different. 
- In commutative diagrams, `\ar@{^{(}->}[r]` is the equivalent for
  `\hookrightarrow` (or `\into`)
